# Rules that change over time (19 Nov 2026)

Who maintains what when a marketplace, carrier, processor or state changes its rules. Fairshop has no rule feed and no
update service: every rule below is a value in a settings file the seller writes and confirms, or a built-in default
that the seller overrides in that file. Each check result lists the settings files it used, with their sha256
(`settings_files`), and says so when it ran on the built-in defaults.

**How a customer updates a rule:** edit the settings JSON for that agent (the README of each agent shows its keys, and
`fixtures/settings.json` or the other fixture JSON files show a complete example), then rerun the check. Nothing is
downloaded and no rule changes on its own. Support (Mythos Machines) can update the built-in defaults in a new release
when a vendor announces a change, but the seller's own settings file always wins.

## Rules that live only in the seller's settings (no built-in value)

The agents ship with no real fees, rates or thresholds for these. The fixture values are fictional and say so.

| Rule | Agent | Settings key (file) | Source to take it from |
|---|---|---|---|
| Marketplace fee schedule (referral %, fixed fees, minimums by category) | fairshop-marketplace-fees | `channels` (`schedule` JSON) | Each marketplace's fee page or your agreement |
| FBA storage rates by season and size tier, aged-inventory surcharge brackets | fairshop-fba-storage-fees | `rates`, `aged_brackets` (`settings`) | Amazon's current FBA fee pages |
| Sales tax: registrations, combined rate per state, whether shipping is taxed, marketplace facilitator channels, exemption certificates | fairshop-sales-tax | `registered`, `marketplace_facilitator`, `exemption_certificates` (`settings`) | Your registrations and your tax adviser; one combined rate per state (local rates are not modelled) |
| Economic nexus thresholds (sales, transactions, "or" or "both", period, marketplace sales counted) | fairshop-nexus-thresholds | `thresholds`, `registered` (`settings`) | Each state's published threshold, confirmed with your adviser |
| Reserve terms (percent, days) per processor | fairshop-processor-reserves | `processors` (`terms` JSON) | Your processor's reserve notice |
| Payout timing and fee caps per channel | fairshop-payouts | `payout_days`, `max_fee_pct` (`settings`) | Your payout schedule and agreements |
| MAP prices, exempt sellers, pauses | fairshop-map-pricing | `map`, `exempt_sellers`, `pause` (`policy` JSON) | Your own MAP policy |
| Affiliate commission rates | fairshop-affiliate-commissions | `default_rate_pct`, `rates` (`terms` JSON) | Your program terms |
| 3PL rate card | fairshop-3pl-invoices | `rates` (`ratecard` JSON) | Your 3PL agreement |
| Retailer allowances and dispute windows per retailer | fairshop-retailer-deductions | `allowances`, `dispute_window_days` (`settings`) | Each retailer's vendor agreement |
| Settlement account map | fairshop-settlement-journals | `accounts`, `clearing_account`, `suspense_account` (`map` JSON) | Your chart of accounts |
| Channel listing rules (title limits, required fields, images) | fairshop-product-data | `channels` (`settings`) | Each channel's listing rules |
| Signature rule for high-value orders | fairshop-marketplace-claims | `signature_over` (`settings`) | Your own shipping policy and the marketplace's claim rules |

## Business choices, not outside rules

Tolerances, review thresholds and windows that are the seller's own choice: payout tolerance (0.05), deposit lag
(cash 2 days, settlements 10 days), shrink target (1.5%), margin floor (15%), return-rate threshold (15%, 10 units),
repeat counts, lead and safety days for stock, PO price and over-receipt tolerances, ROAS target (3), retry rules for
subscriptions, landed-cost spread basis. Each is a settings key with its default named in the agent's README.
