# Real public data (19 Nov 2026)

Made by `node scripts/realdata.mjs --download` (then `node scripts/realdata.mjs` to rerun without the network). The script downloads public files into `_realdata/` (git-ignored, never committed), converts them where needed, runs the agents, and re-counts each result with its own code, written separately from the agents'. Raw numbers: [`realdata-2026-11-19.json`](realdata-2026-11-19.json). No private data was used. Downloaded on 19 Nov 2026.

Four agents were run on public data. For the other 26 (payouts, marketplace fees, FBA reimbursements and storage fees, processor reserves, settlements, marketplace claims, refunds, coupons, gift cards, subscriptions, affiliates, sales tax, nexus, stockouts, MAP prices, SKU margins, ad spend, shipping labels, carrier adjustments, 3PL invoices, purchase orders, landed cost, retailer deductions, cash drawers and shrink) no public file in a real format exists: they need a seller's own account exports, and no vendor publishes a real one. Those 26 agents were tested on fictional files only (see [`accuracy-2026-11-19.md`](accuracy-2026-11-19.md)).

## What changed because of it

- **Product data:** two new layouts (Shopify's current product export, WooCommerce's product export) marked "run on the vendor's sample file"; Shopify product columns filled down by handle in both Shopify layouts; image counts per product; no weight asked of products that do not ship (Shopify `Requires shipping` false, WooCommerce virtual or external); WooCommerce variations take the parent's weight, description and images when blank; barcode cells such as `ean:…; upc:…` and hyphenated ISBNs read; UPC-E accepted.
- **Inventory and listings:** stock rows and listings with no SKU reported once by name (`stock_no_sku`, `listing_no_sku`).
- **Return reasons:** `ignore_skus` setting for line codes that are not products.
- **Core:** a `prepare` hook lets an export layout see the raw rows before columns are mapped; export layouts can now say they were run on a vendor's published sample.

## Limits

- The UCI shop's file has no return reasons, so the reason groups and the "not as described" check were not exercised on real data. Its cancellations are not the same thing as returns; they stand in for them here.
- The Shopify and WooCommerce files are the vendors' own samples, not exports from a live store. They prove the layouts and the fill-down rules, not every column a real store fills.
- Open Food Facts is a food database, not a store catalogue; it was used for its real barcodes.
