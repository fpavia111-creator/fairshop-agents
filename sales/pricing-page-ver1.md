# Fairshop pricing (ver 1)

The software is free (MIT). You pay a fixed price for the setup: we install the agents on your computer, map your exports (Shopify, WooCommerce, BigCommerce, Amazon Seller Central, eBay, Etsy, Walmart Marketplace, Stripe, PayPal, Square, ShipStation, Shippo, your 3PL's invoices and your ad platforms), write your settings with the person who will confirm them, run the first reports with you and train one person on your team.

Fee schedules, reserve terms, rate cards, MAP lists and claim windows come from your own agreements. Sales tax rates, marketplace facilitator rules and nexus thresholds are settings you confirm with your tax adviser: we do not tell you what a state or a marketplace requires, and no agent files a return.

Kurt approved using these prices.

## Support after setup

$200 a month for one pack, $450 a month for the whole shop, $250 a month per brand or per client for multi-brand businesses and partner rollouts. Support covers export changes when a marketplace or app changes its columns, settings updates when a fee schedule or carrier term changes, new versions, and a monthly check-in.

## What is and is not included

Included: installation on your computer, column mapping for your exports, settings written with you, one training session, the first reports.

Not included: refunding, charging, paying, repricing, changing listings or discount codes, filing tax returns, registering with a state, opening marketplace cases, contacting carriers, suppliers, retailers or customers, or logging in to any of your systems. The agents draft; a named person on your team decides and acts. Nothing here is legal, tax or accounting advice.

Your files and reports stay on your computer. Shopper names, e-mail addresses, phone numbers, addresses and staff names are masked in anything sent to the AI model.
